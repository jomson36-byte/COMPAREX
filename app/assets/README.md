# COMPAREX Logo Assets

## Contents

- `svg/comparex-symbol.svg` — primary color symbol
- `svg/comparex-symbol-mono.svg` — monochrome symbol
- `svg/comparex-horizontal-dark.svg` — horizontal logo for light backgrounds
- `svg/comparex-horizontal-light.svg` — horizontal logo for dark backgrounds
- `svg/comparex-wordmark-dark.svg` — wordmark for light backgrounds
- `svg/comparex-wordmark-light.svg` — wordmark for dark backgrounds
- `favicon.svg` and `favicon.ico` — browser favicon
- `png/` — transparent PNG exports at common sizes

## Brand colors

- Deep Ink `#111827`
- Paper White `#F8FAFC`
- Compare Teal `#14B8A6`
- Teal Dark `#0D9488`
- Change Blue `#3B82F6`
- Blue Dark `#2563EB`

## Usage

- Use the dark logo on white or light neutral backgrounds.
- Use the light logo on Deep Ink or near-black backgrounds.
- Keep clear space around the logo equal to at least one quarter of the symbol width.
- Minimum symbol size: 16 px digitally.
- Minimum horizontal logo width: 120 px digitally.
- Do not recolor Version A as green-success or Version B as red-error.
- Do not add gradients, glow, shadows, outlines, or rotation.

The SVG wordmark references Inter/Manrope with Arial fallback. Convert text to outlines in a vector editor before sending files to print.
